Put an app on your iPhone home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap the app you want.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Do this once for Flow and once for Plate. Your entries stay only on this phone, so use Export in each app now and then to keep a backup.
